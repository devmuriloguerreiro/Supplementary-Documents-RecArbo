# Supplementary Documents – RecArbo

This repository provides supplementary documents and datasets associated with the experimental evaluation of **RecArbo**, a context-aware recommender system proposed to support the prevention and response to urban arboviruses.

The materials available in this repository complement the experimental procedures described in the doctoral thesis:

> **Exploiting a Contextual Recommendation Strategy to Support the Prevention and Response to Urban Arboviruses Outbreaks**

by **Murilo Guerreiro Arouca**, under the supervision of **Prof. Dr. Frederico Araújo Durão**, at the **Postgraduate Program in Computer Science (PGCOMP), Federal University of Bahia (UFBA)**.

---

## About RecArbo

RecArbo is a context-aware recommender system designed to integrate heterogeneous contextual information to support the identification and communication of localized risks associated with urban arboviruses.

The system integrates:

- environmental reports collected through crowdsourcing;
- climatic information;
- geographic and spatial information;
- spatio-temporal clustering;
- epidemiological risk assessment; and
- context-aware recommendation of preventive messages.

The system employs **ST-DBSCAN** to identify spatio-temporal clusters and combines environmental and climatic indicators to estimate the risk associated with each cluster. Recommendations are retrieved using semantic representations and subsequently re-ranked according to the contextual characteristics of the identified scenario.

---

## Repository Purpose

This repository contains supplementary materials used in the evaluation and validation of the RecArbo system.

The materials are intended to support:

1. the reproducibility and transparency of the evaluation procedures;
2. inspection of the data collected during the evaluations;
3. analysis of expert assessments;
4. comparison between expert risk classifications and RecArbo outputs; and
5. documentation of the experimental validation protocol.

## Repository Contents

The repository currently contains the following materials:

### `Avaliacao_Experimental_RecArbo.csv`

Dataset containing the responses collected during the experimental evaluation of the RecArbo system.

The experimental evaluation was conducted using historical epidemiological scenarios. Specialists were presented with contextual information describing each scenario and subsequently evaluated the risk classification and the contextual appropriateness of the recommendations generated by RecArbo.

---

### `Avaliacao_por_Especialistas_Portugues.csv`

Dataset containing responses from the expert-based evaluation conducted in Portuguese.

This material corresponds to the evaluation of the proposed recommendation architecture by specialists and contains the assessments collected during the study.

---

### `Avaliação Experimental do Sistema RecArbo...`

Document containing the experimental evaluation instrument and/or supporting material used during the validation of the RecArbo system.

The material describes the scenarios, contextual information and questions presented to the specialists.

---

### `Avaliação por Especialistas (Português) - Google...`

Supporting material associated with the expert-based evaluation conducted in Portuguese.

The document contains the evaluation questions and materials presented to participating specialists.

---

### `RecArbo_Experimental_Scenarios.json`

JSON file containing the historical spatio-temporal scenarios used in the
experimental validation of RecArbo. Each scenario includes its spatial and
temporal characteristics, environmental and climatic indicators, calculated
risk score, risk level, and the preventive recommendations generated by the
system.
